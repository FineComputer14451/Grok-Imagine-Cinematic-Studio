## Description
Turn a one-line concept into an original, production-ready game mascot: one full-body character with a strong iconic silhouette, a memorable color identity, and tons of personality — centered on a keyable (transparent) background, sprite-base ready. Pairs naturally with Character Sprite Maker to animate your mascot into engine-ready sprite sheets.
