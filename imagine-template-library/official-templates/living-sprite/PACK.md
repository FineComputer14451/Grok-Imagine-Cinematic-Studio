## Description
Describe any character and its weapon, pick a motion, and get the full sprite chain in one run: an on-model base render, an image-to-video motion clip, an engine-ready sprite sheet of evenly-aligned frames on a transparent background, and a looping GIF preview - for Unity, Godot, or Phaser. Works across any weapon and body type (bows, staves, axes, guns, wings and more).
