# World kit workflow

Official Image 2.0 pattern: one world from a handful of prompts — a character, her locations, and the props she carries — generated separately, holding one style.

## Steps
1. Pick style pack (or lock grade in DNA/bible)
2. Mint/lock character heroes (`char_<slug>_hero_*`) on Image 2.0 Quality
3. Mint location establish + coverage (empty of characters)
4. Mint prop hero / reverse / detail
5. Multi-ref compose: up to 5 refs (char + loc + props) for scene stills
6. Optional: BG-remove char/prop → composite onto location
7. Smart-resize for delivery, then Photo → Video if needed
