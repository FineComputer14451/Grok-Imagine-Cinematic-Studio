# Camera Erotic Vocabulary

Lens, movement, and framing per erotic phase.

---

## By Phase

### Anticipation
- **Move:** static hold → slow dolly in
- **Lens:** 50mm f/1.4
- **Framing:** MCU, negative space between bodies
- **Pacing:** Hold 3–4s before any move

### Approach
- **Move:** slow dolly in (2cm/s), orbit 15°
- **Lens:** 35mm f/1.8
- **Framing:** OTS intimate, profile two-shot
- **Pacing:** Deliberate — never rush first contact

### Contact
- **Move:** slow orbit, macro skin detail
- **Lens:** 85mm f/1.4
- **Framing:** Close on contact point — hands, lips, collarbone
- **Pacing:** One contact focus per clip

### Escalation
- **Move:** tracking medium, dolly follows momentum
- **Lens:** 40mm anamorphic feel
- **Framing:** Medium full, body lines, motivated shadows
- **Pacing:** Camera follows physics — never fights body

### Peak
- **Move:** hero push-in, optional 80% slow-mo
- **Lens:** 50mm or 85mm (locked for sequence)
- **Framing:** Single body focus or intimate two-shot
- **Pacing:** One unbroken beat, no internal cuts

### Afterglow
- **Move:** static pull back, slow dolly out
- **Lens:** 35mm f/2
- **Framing:** Wider — context returns
- **Pacing:** Motion −40%, hold final 2s
