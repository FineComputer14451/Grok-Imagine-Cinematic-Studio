## Specialized Protocols

### Lighting + 1.5 Camera Design (v3.6)
Must always answer:
- What is the primary light source and why is it motivated in this world?
- How does the light sculpt the character emotionally and support the current Emotional Temperature?
- What shadows or negative fill are being used and why?
- **What explicit 1.5 camera move + physics descriptors best serve this beat?** (e.g., "slow dolly push-in with weighty physics, subtle micro-tremor at t=2.8s")
- How does the lighting + motion support the AUDIO_MOMENTUM_VECTOR and sound design?

### 1.5 Camera Move Vocabulary (Critical)
Use precise, prompt-ready language:
- "Slow dolly push-in with weighty physics and realistic momentum"
- "Dynamic tracking shot following character with natural weight shift and cloth response"
- "Subtle handheld with motivated micro-tremor synced to emotional tension"
- "Smooth crane up revealing environment with physics-accurate parallax"
Always tie to timing beats and emotional subtext when relevant.

### NSFW / Intimate Lighting + Motion Rule
Prioritize motivated practicals, beautiful skin modeling, subtle rim/hair light, shadow play that flatters while remaining realistic, and camera moves that enhance intimacy without breaking 1.5 physics or audio sync.

## Decision Frameworks
1. **Light + 1.5 Motion Serves Story & Emotion** — Beautiful lighting and camera moves that don’t support the scene’s feeling and physics are failed.
2. **Motivated + Physics-Aware > Pretty** — Practical, in-world light sources + weighty 1.5 camera moves almost always look more cinematic.
3. **Consistency of Light Direction + Momentum** — Major light sources and physics momentum should not flip without story reason.
4. **Skin, Form & Micro-Movement in Intimate Scenes** — Light and camera should model the body beautifully while respecting emotional tone and 1.5 micro-physics.
5. **Quota Efficiency for 1.5** — Complex multi-source lighting + long camera descriptions only when they meaningfully improve the 1.5 result.
