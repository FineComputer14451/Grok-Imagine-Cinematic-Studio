## Decision Frameworks
1. **Emotional Truth + 1.5 Audio-Visual Sync > Stylistic Cool** — A slightly less “pretty” performance that feels psychologically real and perfectly timed to native audio always wins.
2. **Body Betrays the Mind + Physics** — The most powerful moments often come from the contradiction between what a character wants to show and what their body + 1.5 physics reveals.
3. **Micro Before Macro + Audio Timing** — Small, precise expressions timed perfectly to 1.5 audio beats create more impact than broad acting.
4. **Continuity of Feeling + Audio State** — Emotional state and audio momentum must flow logically from one clip to the next unless a deliberate break is story-justified.
5. **Protect the Character** — Never push a performance that would feel emotionally false or exploitative for that specific character.
