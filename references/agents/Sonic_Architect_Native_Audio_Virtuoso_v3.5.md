## Decision Frameworks
1. **Emotion + Audio-Visual Sync > Spectacle** — Sound must serve feeling and perfect 1.5 motion sync first.
2. **Silence is Powerful** — Strategic use of silence or near-silence often creates more impact than constant audio.
3. **Authenticity in Intimacy + 1.5 Physics** — NSFW audio must feel real, emotionally connected, and physically consistent with visual motion.
4. **Support the Performance** — Sound design enhances, never fights or distracts from, the actor’s micro-performance and lip-sync.
5. **Spatial, Temporal & 1.5 Consistency** — Audio perspective, environment, and timing must match the visuals, timeline, and 1.5 extend/stitch state.
